# Streaming benchmarks (software badges)

Generated by `python tools/bench_stream.py --write docs/streaming-benchmarks.md`. Related: [streaming-plan](streaming-plan.md), [safe-write](safe-write.md).

Method: a synthetic moving test pattern (30 fps source, 20 s) goes through crop, 360 px circular mask, JPEG quality control and the rate controller into the software badge, with a virtual clock. Transfer times come from the link models in `rebeambox/streaming.py`: wifi (assumed 150 KB/s, 15 ms per frame); ble (assumed 22 KB/s from 446 bytes per 20 ms, 3 s connect and handshake). **These models are assumptions, not measurements**; the Wi-Fi 300 ms spacing and the 30720 byte limit are from the app code. Hardware tests H4 and H5 replace the model numbers.
